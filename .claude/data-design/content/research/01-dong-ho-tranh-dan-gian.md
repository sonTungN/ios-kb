# Đông Hồ — Nghề làm tranh dân gian (the living-heritage subject)

**Play pattern: transmit to survive.** The player is a craft household. Every era offers a better
living outside the craft, and the run is won by what reaches the next generation, not by what the
workshop earns. The correct player arrives poor — which is, verbatim, why UNESCO inscribed the craft
in 2025: *"the number of skilled and committed individuals pursuing Đông Hồ printing as a livelihood
has become too small to sustain transmission."*

> ⚠️ This file is a **research scaffold**, not content. Built in three passes (timeline & heritage
> status · craft process & customs · paintings & revival), Vietnamese sources primary. It cites
> **17 URLs across 15 domains** below, and names **28 distinct outlets** in the per-claim source
> columns. Every claim carries a status: ✅ = 2+ independent sources ·
> ⚠️ `VERIFY` = single source · ❌ = sources contradict (both readings recorded — pick the safe
> wording, never silently resolve). Card text is written by the team, from sources, in vi + en.
> Method note: part of the pass ran on WebSearch/WebFetch during a Firecrawl outage; source list at
> the bottom is what was actually consulted.

## The craft — process facts (for cards, minigames, and the codex)

**Paper.** Giấy dó from vỏ cây dó (bark soaked/cooked in lime ~3 months, pounded, sheet-formed with a
liềm seo screen) — bought from **làng Đống Cao** (= Dương Ổ, Phong Khê, ~12 km away; "huyện Yên
Phong" in older sources is the same village's earlier administration; VHNT also names làng Hồ Khẩu
ven Hồ Tây as a second dó source) ✅. Period naming: pre-1963 prose may say "Quảng Yên" for the
điệp coast — verify before printing the period name. **Giấy điệp**: crushed vỏ
con điệp (shells from Quảng Ninh, lime-cured 1–2 years ⚠️ detail), mixed with **hồ** (rice paste —
tẻ/nếp/sắn variants all documented), brushed on with the **thét** (chổi lá thông, pine-needle brush,
made mainly in làng Đạo Tú) — the ridged strokes ("ganh") and shell glitter are the signature
shimmering ground ✅.

**Pigments — the five "thuốc cái", all natural** (each color's material matters: it is codex + card
content):

| Màu | Material | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trắng | vỏ điệp (crushed shell) | ✅ all sources |
| Đen | **than lá tre** (bamboo leaves burnt, cured months); variant rơm nếp ✅; than gỗ xoan ⚠️ wiki-only |
| Xanh | **lá chàm** (indigo, from highland Tày/Nùng trade) ✅; gỉ đồng ⚠️ wiki-only |
| Vàng | **hoa hòe** (roasted, boiled) ✅ all sources; hoa dành dành supplement ✅ (note: *hoa*, not "hạt") |
| Đỏ | **sỏi son/đá son** (hillside red stone; "núi Thiên Thai" localization ⚠️ wiki-only) + **gỗ vang** (sappanwood, boiled) ✅; ❗ dsvh.gov.vn (state record) instead names **hoa hiên** — coexisting traditions, record both, never silently resolve |

Colors intermix (vang + phèn chua → đỏ tía; chàm + hòe → hoa lý) and every color is bound with hồ
nếp before printing ✅.

**Blocks & printing.** Ván nét (outline block): **gỗ thị or gỗ thừng mực** ✅ (⚠️ do NOT say thừng
mực is for color blocks — contradicted). Ván màu: a softer wood — species varies by source (gỗ mỡ /
vàng tâm / dổi) — safe wording: "gỗ mềm hơn" ✅. Tools: bộ ve, 30–40 gouges ✅. **One block per color
+ one outline block** ("tranh có bao nhiêu màu, bấy nhiêu ván"; typical sheet 4–5 blocks; tranh bộ
up to 16, tranh chủ up to 24) ✅. **Printing order: colors one at a time, dried between; the black
outline (ván nét) is ALWAYS printed last**, by the most skilled hand ✅ (dsvh + VHNT). Method: the
block is charged from the **bìa** (cloth-faced pad) with the thét, **pressed down onto the sheet**
(register marks "cữ"), flipped with the paper and rubbed with **xơ mướp** (loofah), peeled, dried ✅
— the reverse of Hàng Trống's paper-onto-block habit (implied). Some old blocks had Hán-Nôm
inscriptions chiseled off post-1945 (⚠️) — a documented scar of adaptation.

**Sister traditions (boundary facts, one line each):** Hàng Trống (Hà Nội) = "nửa in nửa vẽ" — one
outline block, colors hand-brushed, urban/worship audience ✅. Kim Hoàng = "tranh đỏ", outline on red
paper, hand-colored, died out ~7 decades, being revived ✅. **Lý ngư vọng nguyệt is famously Hàng
Trống** — Đông Hồ workshops print versions today, but label origin honestly ✅.

**Tết custom** ✅: every household, rich or poor, bought tranh at Tết and pasted the bright sheets on
the wall beside câu đối đỏ — a wish for hòa thuận, ấm no, phúc lộc; **replaced with new sheets each
Tết** (the "pasted over last year's" detail is ⚠️ unconfirmed — say "thay mới mỗi năm"). Today the
custom has faded (wikipedia) — which is exactly the game's stake.

**Education & revival (2013–2026)** ✅: the essay "Tranh Đông Hồ — nét tinh hoa của văn hóa dân gian
Việt Nam" is **Văn bản 1, Bài 4, SGK Ngữ văn 10 tập 1 (Chân trời sáng tạo)** — the craft is literally
in the national curriculum. Experience classes at the private center (school groups print their own
sheets). Áo dài Tết 2025 collections built on Đông Hồ motifs (Võ Việt Chung, Tuổi Trẻ 17/1/2025 ✅ +

nên hay không?"). Prices: mounted Vinh hoa–Phú quý pair 35×45cm ≈ 600k VND at a village e-shop;

## Contradictions & gaps register (never silently resolve; Report §2 material)

1. **Origin century** ❌ 15th–17th across sources → game says "khoảng 500 năm / thời Lê".
2. **Chợ tranh phiên count** ❌ 5 (written majority: 6·11·16·21·26 tháng Chạp) vs 6 (+mồng 1, per
   nghệ nhân Chế, first-person). Use 5 in-card, note 6 in codex.
3. **Village burned "1947"** — event ✅, year unfound. **The game uses:** "trong kháng chiến chống
   Pháp" in every player-facing string. Card 7 carries 1947 as an ordering year only, marked `ᵇ` in
   the level map, and the year is never printed on a card, a chip or the ledger.
4. **Nguyễn Hữu Sam death** ❌ 2016 vs 2017 ("2018" unsupported). Avoid printing a year.
5. **Nguyễn Đăng Chế return** ❌ 1985 vs 1992. Say "từ giữa thập niên 1980, toàn tâm từ 1992" or avoid.
6. **Đỏ pigment** ❌ sỏi son + gỗ vang (VHNT/wiki) vs hoa hiên (dsvh). Record both traditions.
7. **State center cost** ❌ 45.2 tỷ vs "gần 100 tỷ". Avoid the number or date it.
8. **Đám cưới chuột meaning** — satire (mainstream) vs Tết-wish layer (Tạp chí Mỹ thuật). **The
   game uses:** the print is **excluded from `mg_match` grading entirely** — a contested reading
   must not become a right answer — and the codex carries both layers side by side. "Chí chí… mưu
   mưu" inscription = Hàng Trống version.
9. **Số mẫu tranh còn lại** — no total exists; never print a count.
10. **Households remaining** — 2 dòng họ / 3 gia đình / "vài hộ" (UNESCO) — magnitude consistent;
    date any figure used.
11. **Nguyễn Thị Oanh NNND elevation** — unconfirmed (a different Oanh matches); she is NNƯT 2020 ✅.
12. **Tổ nghề Đông Hồ** — no Đông Hồ-specific craft-ancestor figure or feast date could be sourced;
    only the general hội làng 14–16/3 âm lịch surfaced. The card's `historicalNote` therefore speaks
    of craft-ancestor rites as a Vietnamese craft-village custom (`historicity: invented`) — keep it
    generic until a source settles it.
13. Firecrawl outage forced part of the pass onto WebSearch/WebFetch; bacninh.gov.vn and
    svhttdl.bacninh.gov.vn could not be deep-read (served navigation only).
