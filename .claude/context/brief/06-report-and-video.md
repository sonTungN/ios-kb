# Documentation and presentation

## 1 · The report (`Document.pdf`)

**Format:** professionally formatted — cover page, table of contents, page numbers. **Maximum 30 pages**, *excluding* the cover page, table of contents, references, and appendices. Per-feature screenshots may be placed in an appendix and referenced from the body.

**Required sections, in the brief's order:**

1. **Introduction & Context**
   - Application name, purpose, and target audience.
   - Inspiration for the topic and how it relates to the assignment theme.
   - Justification of the topic's relevance and originality within the chosen cultural context.
   - Explanation of why the heritage is worth understanding, experiencing, preserving, or reinterpreting.
   - Explanation of the game's rules and educational goals.
   - In short: clearly explain the game's **"Cultural Element → Gameplay Mechanic → Player Learning"** relationship.
2. **Research and Cultural Representation**
   - How the team researched the selected heritage;
   - Important information discovered during the research process;
   - Regional, historical, linguistic, or community-specific considerations where relevant;
   - How the team attempted to represent the heritage **responsibly and accurately**;
   - Which aspects are **authentic**; which have been **simplified for gameplay**; which are **creative reinterpretations**.
3. **Competitor Analysis**
   - Identify relevant existing products.
   - Describe the app's **"killer feature(s)"** that make it stand out — "a distinctive functionality, gameplay mechanic, educational approach, cultural integration, or user experience that provides a strong reason for a user to choose your application over comparable alternatives."
4. **Implementation Details**
   - Detailed explanation of **all** implemented features (core and advanced).
   - For each feature: a **UI screenshot** + a brief technical explanation of its implementation.
   - **Justify the choice of cloud service and any local persistence mechanisms**, including **why particular data was assigned to each** (Core Data, Firebase, UserDefaults).
5. **Application of Industry Best Practice**
   - Explain whether the implementation follows the recommended best practices from the **Week 7 Guest Lecture**.
   - Identify **specific recommendations** from the lecture and explain how they influenced design or implementation.
   - "Do not simply state that your implementation 'follows best practice.' **Provide evidence and explanation**."
6. **Application Flow Diagram** — illustrating how views and components interact, **designed with the MVVM architecture in mind**.
7. **Technologies Used** — a list of all Swift/SwiftUI components and any external libraries used.
8. **Design and User Experience** — explain design choices (colors, fonts, layout) and how an intuitive, consistent, user-centered design was achieved.
9. **Known Bugs/Problems** — a list of any unresolved issues.
10. **Conclusion** — reflection on what was learned and potential future improvements.
11. **Project Responsibilities** — a table of **each member's actual responsibilities** and **workload (%)** in relation to project subtasks, based on the task breakdown. **Percentages must total 100%.** Identify which member served as **Project Manager** and which as **Technical Lead**. "This table must be discussed and agreed by the whole team and finalised by the Project Manager."
12. **References & Appendices.**


## 2 · The video presentation

- **One single video, max 10 minutes**, uploaded to YouTube as an **"unlisted"** video; URL goes in `Youtube.txt` (`BRIEF-01`).
- Must include **two parts**:
  1. **Project Presentation** — a slide-based presentation where **all team members** explain the project's analysis, design, and implementation, reflecting the report's structure.
  2. **Application Demo** — a live demonstration of the app **showcasing all implemented features**. "This part should be prioritized."
- Time allocation between the parts is up to the team, within the limit — but **"the actual application demo is more important and should be more prioritized, which showcases all of the required features of your app."**
- **All group members must appear and speak** in the recorded video. **"Any member who does not appear, or whose contribution is not meaningful, will receive a mark of zero for the presentation component."**
- **Introduce yourself by name** at the start of your segment so markers can identify each speaker.
- Framed by the brief as an opportunity to build presentation skills — each member should review their work and prepare well before the demo.
