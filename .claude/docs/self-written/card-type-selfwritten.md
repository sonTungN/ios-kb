### Card Type
#### 1. Decision Cards
There are 3 types. Every one presents a **choice**, and every choice has **a value and a tradeoff**.
- **Spine**: Main events, happen in fixed sequence according to the Vietnam's history record. Never shuffled, never skipped. Has 2 types:
	- **Normal**: Normal historic events
		- `setup` (context)
		- `pressure` (tempts you toward the generic path like build the army, tax the people, etc..)
		- `relief` (lets you recover a stat)
		- **`trap`** (looks like the best deal in the level and costs you the run).
	- **Carrier (mini-boss)**: Key events that decide whether you can win. Each grants a **flag** and each carries a **mandatory cost**, along with **required minimum** Player's Stat.
		- **Below the floor, the cost is still paid and the flag is not granted**, but the run stays playable but is no longer winnable
- **Weave**: Original sub-events between the main. The only source of run-to-run variety in the decisions.
	- **Pull random on every fresh run**
	- **NEVER** carries a required flag.
	- _e.g. an old general asks to retire; a magistrate is accused of taking bribes; the prople uniting to contribute food to support the troops, etc..._
- **Crisis**: Crisis events. Appear when **player's stats** enter a danger band
	- *e.g. "than" (Quyền Thần) runs too high -> a minister eclipses the throne. (You must pick how to handle it, and every option costs a different stat)

#### 2. Non-decision Cards
- **Ambient**: Small, positive buffs granted to the player to receive.
- **Interstitial**: Info Card for notification.
	- **Advisor** (what the coming card will cost, put in some fixed position, _off on Khó_)
	- **Omen** (an invasion is coming, one card ahead, fixed position, never off)
	- **Echo** (a callback to a choice you made earlier, the only one conditioned on your decisions).

#### 3. The Final Trial (boss): Not a card.
It asks nothing; it **judges**. Two separate questions, and they are not equal:
- **Did you prepare?** — all required flags. _**This alone decides victory.**_
- **Is there a state left to fight with?** — a **deliberately low floor** (`binh ≥ 15 · dan ≥ 15 · kho ≥ 10 · than ≥ 10`). Clearing it never wins anything. The correct player is _supposed_ to arrive poor.
