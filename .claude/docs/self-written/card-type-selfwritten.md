### Card Type
#### 1. Decision Cards
There are 3 types. Every one presents a **choice**, and every choice has **a value and a tradeoff**.
- **Spine**: Main events, happen in fixed sequence according to the documented record. Never shuffled, never skipped. Has 2 types:
	- **Normal**: Normal historic events
		- `setup` (context)
		- `pressure` (tempts you toward the generic path — take the bulk order, switch the workshop to vàng mã, print the "cải tiến" design)
		- `relief` (lets you recover a stat)
		- **`trap`** (looks like the best deal on the table and costs you the run).
	- **Carrier (mini-boss)**: Key events that decide whether you can win. Each grants a **flag** and each carries a **mandatory cost**, along with **required minimum** Player's Stat.
		- **Below the floor, the cost is still paid and the flag is not granted** — the run stays playable, but is no longer winnable
- **Weave**: Original sub-events between the main. The only source of run-to-run variety in the decisions.
	- **Pull random on every fresh run**
	- **NEVER** carries a required flag.
	- _e.g. a wedding orders prints and asks what the picture means; a daughter asks to stand at the block; the guild borrows a woodblock; a reporter wants a day in the workshop._
- **Crisis**: Crisis events. Appear when **player's stats** enter a danger band
	- *e.g. `tiếng` runs too high -> orders flood in faster than the hand can print, and fakes appear under the village name. (You must pick how to handle it, and every option costs a different stat)

#### 2. Non-decision Cards
- **Ambient**: Small, positive buffs granted to the player to receive.
- **Interstitial**: Info Card for notification.
	- **Advisor** (what the coming card will cost, in fixed positions before the floored carriers, _off on Khó_)
	- **Omen** (what is coming — the war reaching the district, the offset calendars, the survey team — one card ahead, fixed position, never off)
	- **Echo** (a callback to a choice you made earlier, the only one conditioned on your decisions).
	- **Chapter** (the era turn made visible — era title, one line of bridge text, a ledger snapshot; fixed before cards 13 and 23, never off).

#### 3. The Final Trial (boss): Not a card.
It asks nothing; it **judges**. Two separate questions, and they are not equal:
- **Did you prepare?** — all required flags. _**This alone decides victory.**_
- **Is there a household left to act with?** — a **deliberately low floor** (`nghề ≥ 15 · sinh kế ≥ 10 · tiếng ≥ 10 · người ≥ 15`). Clearing it never wins anything. The correct player is _supposed_ to arrive poor.
