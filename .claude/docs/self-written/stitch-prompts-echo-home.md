## 1. "Dư âm" (Echo) event screen

"Dư âm" (Echo) event screen for the Dong Ho Art card game, iPhone portrait, Vietnamese UI, giấy điệp paper background #F0E6CB, đỏ son #B63A2E accents, chàm #31536A, than tre #20352D outlines, Noto Serif titles, Be Vietnam Pro text. Flat matte colours, hand-carved outlines, small offset ink shadows, no gradients, no pure white, no stat bar, no top navigation bar.

An Echo is a consequence returning from a decision the player made several cards ago. The screen is a full-screen interstitial, read only, one button.

Layout top to bottom:
1. A small stamp pill at top left reading "DƯ ÂM" in đỏ son, with a faint ink ripple motif (three thin concentric arcs) beside it, and a small caption at top right "Lá 5 · 1940".
2. A "memory card": a slightly tilted, faded miniature of the earlier decision card, drawn like a woodblock print pinned to paper with a paper tab. It shows the earlier card's title "Đói ất dậu" and year "1945" in small type, with a folded corner ribbon reading "Nhà đã chọn: Chia gạo cho làng" in đỏ son. The card looks aged (paper fibre texture, slightly lower contrast) to read as the past.
3. A large passage panel with a thin indigo rule and a quotation mark ornament: the echo text, 3 to 4 lines, Be Vietnam Pro 17px: "Những nhà được chia gạo năm ấy nay trả ơn: mùa in này họ đến giúp không công, tay khắc vẫn còn nhớ nhịp." Under it a small italic line "Việc đã làm quay lại đúng lúc." in muted ink.
4. A line of three tiny folk print ornaments (lotus, carp, chick) as a divider.
5. At the bottom, in the flow (not pinned): a primary button "Tiếp tục" in đỏ son with hard offset shadow, full width, 44px tall; under it a small uppercase footer "DƯ ÂM · KHÔNG ĐỔI CHỈ SỐ" in muted ink.

Respect iPhone safe areas and 20px margins. No copied historical artwork, fictional people only, no text inside illustrations.

## 2. Home ("Trang chủ") redesign

Redesign of the Home screen ("Trang chủ") for the Dong Ho Art card game, iPhone portrait, Vietnamese UI, creative and intuitive, warm giấy điệp paper background #F0E6CB, đỏ son #B63A2E primary, chàm #31536A, lá chàm #315E50, vàng điệp #D6A437, than tre #20352D outlines, Noto Serif titles, Be Vietnam Pro text. Flat matte colour blocks, hand-carved outlines, slightly imperfect ink edges, small offset ink shadows, tiny folk print ornaments, no gradients, no pure white, no corporate blue.

The home is the hub of a three-chapter journey through a Đông Hồ woodblock craft household from 1938 to 2025. It must include every control of the current app:
- Top bar: the wordmark "Dong Ho Art" as a carved stamp, and a gear icon button top right (quick settings). No back button.
- A hero band: an original woodblock style illustration of a craft village in a wide band (a printing yard with drying prints, fictional people, no text), with the slogan "Giữ lửa · Giữ nếp · Hồi sinh" over a paper label.
- "Hành trình đang dở" card, the current gameplay preview: chapter name "Chương II · Giữ nếp", a progress line "Lá 4/10" with a carved progress bar, a difficulty chip "Thường" that can be tapped, and one big primary button "Tiếp tục hành trình". Do NOT show any stat numbers in this card, no Craft/Livelihood/Standing/People values.
- A "Làng nghề" row: a featured tile "Tranh Đông Hồ · 1 làng đang mở" with a small print thumbnail and a button "Chọn làng nghề", plus two greyed "sắp mở" tiles (Gốm, Quan họ) as teasers.
- A secondary row of two outlined buttons: "Cách chơi" (how to play, book icon) and "Khởi động lại" (restart, counterclockwise arrow icon, in a cautious outlined đỏ son style).
- Bottom paper tab bar with three tabs: "Trang chủ" (active, stamped in đỏ son with a hard offset shadow), "Xếp hạng", "Hồ sơ".

Make the hierarchy obvious: continue the journey is the single loudest action; choosing a village is second; restart is quiet. Use paper cards with carved borders and 8px corner radius. Respect iPhone safe areas, 20px margins, 44px touch targets. Fictional people only, no text inside illustrations.
