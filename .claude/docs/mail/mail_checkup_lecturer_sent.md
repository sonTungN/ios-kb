We are from Group 1. My team is planning our Assignment 2 iOS game and would like to ask for your feedback before we begin full development.

Our proposed game is an educational swipe-card strategy game about Vietnamese living heritage, focusing first on the Trần dynasty and the resistance against the Yuan-Mongol invasions. The player takes the role of the Đại Việt court and must survive a sequence of historical decisions leading toward the final battle at Bạch Đằng.

The core gameplay is based on difficult two-choice cards. Each card presents a historical or political situation, and the player swipes left or right to make a decision. Every choice affects four court stats: military strength, popular support, treasury, and court stability. However, the player cannot win simply by maximizing a single stat. If a stat becomes too low or too high, the dynasty can collapse in a historically meaningful way, such as lack of troops, public unrest, an empty treasury, or powerful ministers overtaking the court.

The game will include several types of cards to create variety. Main historical cards follow the real timeline of the Trần dynasty, while key “preparation” cards act like mini-boss moments where the player can secure important historical advantages. There will also be crisis cards when a stat becomes dangerous, random court-event cards to create replayability, and support cards such as advisor, omen, and echo cards that warn, foreshadow, or show the delayed consequences of earlier decisions.

The main feature of our game is that victory depends on historical preparation, not raw power. To win the Bạch Đằng final trial, the player must secure key preparations inspired by real history, such as national unity from Diên Hồng, the scorched-earth strategy of abandoning Thăng Long, and the Bạch Đằng river trap. These choices are costly when made, so the player experiences the tension of sacrificing short-term resources for long-term survival.

We hope this makes Vietnamese history part of the game system itself. The player learns that Đại Việt’s victory was not only about military strength, but also about leadership, unity, logistics, timing, and strategic sacrifice.

Could you please let us know whether this concept is suitable for the “Living Heritage - Vietnam: Culture in Play” theme? We would also appreciate your advice on the project’s scope, especially whether focusing on one polished Trần dynasty level with clear stages is acceptable, or whether we should implement multiple dynasties for progression.

Thank you very much for your time and feedback.
