My main concern is the theme alignment. The project currently focuses heavily on military and dynastic history, particularly the Trần dynasty and the resistance against the Yuan-Mongol invasions. While this is an important part of Vietnamese history, it is not clearly connected to the idea of "living heritage".

I would encourage the team to brainstorm how the game could better reflect practices, traditions, knowledge, customs, crafts, stories, festivals, or cultural elements that continue to influence Vietnamese life today. The historical setting could still be part of the game, but the heritage aspect should play a stronger role in the core experience.

Happy to discuss this further during the tutorial and help you shape the idea into something that fits the theme more closely.
